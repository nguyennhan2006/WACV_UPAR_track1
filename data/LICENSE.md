# License of the data products in `data/`

The files in this folder are derived from the UPAR annotations:

- `split_0710/`: lists of training-image names for our train/validation split;
- `cmask/`: flags marking training labels that our out-of-fold models disagreed with, and per-attribute counts.

The UPAR annotations are published under the Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 3.0 license (CC BY-NC-SA 3.0, https://github.com/speckean/upar_dataset). Under its share-alike term, these derived files are released under the same license:

- Attribution: cite the UPAR papers (see the main README, Section 10) and this repository.
- NonCommercial: no commercial use.
- ShareAlike: works derived from these files must use the same license.
